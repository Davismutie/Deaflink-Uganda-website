Who we are

Our name signifies connection.

Deaf Link Uganda (DLU) was founded in October 2007 by Nassozi B. Kiyaga, beginning as a community-based organisation working with Deaf and hard-of-hearing people in Uganda's Central region. In 2017 it was upgraded to a national NGO to widen its reach.

DLU focuses on the social model of deafness, recognising the uniqueness and potential of each individual. Our name signifies our desire to link Deaf and hard-of-hearing people across Uganda, Africa, and the world — connections that celebrate human diversity and unify Deaf and hearing communities.

Our Vision

Promote inclusion and enable Deaf and hard-of-hearing people to realise their full potential so as to lead productive and dignified lives.

Our Mission

Create access to education, economic, social and cultural opportunities that advance the welfare of Deaf and hard-of-hearing people in Uganda.

Our Logo

The triangle represents a Deaf child rising from a strong, nurturing foundation of family and community. The encircling arrow is the link that connects us all as one human race.

What we do

Three programmes, one cross-cutting commitment.

Cultural and socio-economic barriers keep many Deaf Ugandans from participating as equal citizens. DLU responds with three core programmes — with Deaf Rights & Advocacy woven through all of them.

Education

Closing the access gap

Uganda Sign Language (USL) was recognised as the Deaf community's official language in 1995, yet an estimated 80% of Deaf children in Uganda are still denied full access to education. DLU works to change that.

Read more →
Business Enterprise

Economic self-reliance

Supporting skilled Deaf youth and adults into self-employment and enterprise, including the Deaf Business Group in Mityana set up to secure employment for skilled Deaf youth.

Read more →
Livelihoods

Reaching rural communities

Improving the lives and welfare of Deaf people, their families, and communities — particularly in remote rural areas where marginalisation and poverty are most common.

Read more →

Deaf Rights & Advocacy — cutting across every programme

DLU works within Uganda's national disability movement to challenge stigma, raise awareness, and advocate for accessible services — joining the Uganda National Association of the Deaf each year for International Deaf Awareness Week.
